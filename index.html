    — ANSWERS —

    FAQ

    Is the fitment tool actually free? ↓

    Yes. Forever. No login required, no email gate, no credit card. Buy us a coffee on Instagram if you want.

    Do you take a commission on sales? ↓

    No. Sellers keep 100%. We tag the seller in every post and the buyer contacts them directly.

    What chassis do you cover? ↓

    Every BMW M model from the E30 M3 (1986) through the G87 M2, G80 M3, G82 M4, G90 M5, and F92 M8. M-SUVs and the XM are coming in Phase 2.

    How accurate is the fitment AI? ↓

    It's trained on a database compiled from Apex Wheels, BimmerTips, Fitment Industries, Wheel-Size, and BimmerPost — the sources every serious BMW enthusiast already trusts. Always test-fit before final mounting; tire brand and ride height matter.

    Can I sell wheels here that aren't BMW? ↓

    Right now we're BMW M only — that's the niche. If we expand, you'll hear about it on @mspecwheels first.
